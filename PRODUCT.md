# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (existing codebase). Hosted as static files and opened from a shared link.

## Users

One reader: Ireti, the sender's partner. She opens it on her phone from a link sent to her, alone and unhurried, and may come back to it again later.

## Product Purpose

A private, timeless love letter from King Gem Gem to Ireti. It is not tied to a date or occasion; it exists so she can open it on any day and be reminded she is loved. Success: she reads all of it, lingers on the photos and reasons, and wants to come back.

## Positioning

Made by hand for one person: their own photos, their own reasons, their own passcode (the day they got together). Nothing about it is generic or reusable for anyone else.

## Operating Context

- Opened on a phone via a link, so first impression is the link preview and a small portrait screen.
- Passcode gate first (code `1803`, hint: "the day we finally got together"), then the card.
- Background music plays once she interacts; she must be able to pause it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Passcode gate (client-side only; it keeps casual eyes out, not determined ones).
- The letter itself.
- Memories timeline of photos with soft, undated captions. Do not invent dates or events.
- "Reasons" carousel (10 reasons, see `script.js`).
- Background music (`assets/music/song.mp3`) with a play/pause control.

## Brand Commitments

- Signed "King Gem Gem".
- Voice: warm, sincere, a little playful (one reason is an inside joke: "Because you gave me something ;) ❤."). Not grandiose.
- Direction chosen by the sender: a mix of refined rose and scrapbook.

## Evidence on Hand

- `assets/images/passcode.jpeg`: their hands held together.
- `assets/images/photo.jpeg`: selfie of them together in a lab.
- `assets/images/memory-selfie.jpeg`: selfie of them together by a "Preparatory Room" sign.
- `assets/music/song.mp3`: the chosen song.
- No dates, events or quotes beyond these; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. It should read like it came from him, not from a template.
2. The photos and words are the content; decoration serves them.
3. Calm and unhurried on a phone: nothing should compete with reading.
4. Timeless: no wording that dates it to a month or occasion.
